# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-24)

- Schedule times for the Deck to go to sleep and to wake up, each at the next time the clock shows that time.
- The plugin panel lists upcoming events with how long until each one runs, and each can be edited or cleared.
- Sleep works like pressing the power button. A toast warns a minute before, and clearing the event cancels it.
- Wake turns the Deck back on from sleep. It can't turn on a Deck that is shut down.
